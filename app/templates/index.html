Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about booking, seat locking, and web check-in.

How does CloudSky prevent double seat bookings?

When you select a seat, our database places an atomic row-level lock with a 5-minute timeout. No other user can select or checkout with that seat until your session completes or expires.

When can I perform Web Check-in?

Web check-in opens 48 hours prior to your scheduled departure and remains open until 60 minutes before takeoff. You receive an instant printable boarding pass with a verified QR code.

How can I change or cancel my reservation?

Visit the Manage Booking portal using your 6-character PNR code and passenger surname. You can modify seat assignments or cancel with instant refund computation based on your fare class.

What baggage allowance is included in my ticket?

Economy Classic includes 7 kg cabin baggage and 15 kg check-in baggage. Business Class and First Sanctuary enjoy expanded allowances up to 40 kg with priority handling.
