Important Dangerous Goods Regulations

Power Banks & Lithium Batteries

Must be carried in CABIN baggage only. Strictly prohibited in checked luggage under DGCA safety protocols.

Liquids, Aerosols & Gels (LAGs)

In cabin baggage, containers must not exceed 100 ml each and should fit in a single transparent re-sealable bag.

Restricted Sharp Objects

Scissors, blades, matchboxes, and compressed gas canisters are prohibited in cabin bags and must be checked in.

24/7 PASSENGER SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions & Special Assistance

Find quick answers to common questions about booking, online check-in, seat reservations, and special passenger care.

When does online Web Check-in open and close?

Web check-in opens 48 hours prior to the scheduled departure time and closes 60 minutes prior for domestic flights (75 minutes for international flights). You can complete web check-in and download your digital boarding pass directly via our Web Check-in Portal.

How does the 5-Minute Seat Reservation Hold work?

When you select a seat on our interactive 2D cabin map, our database locks it with a temporary reservation token for 5 minutes (300 seconds). During this window, no other passenger can book or select your held seat while you complete passenger details and payment authorization.

How do I retrieve or reprint my Boarding Pass?

You can retrieve and reprint your official airline boarding pass at any time by visiting our Manage Booking Portal. Simply enter your 6-character PNR code (e.g., CS-7K9A2M) and your registered email address.

Can I swap or change my seat after booking?

Yes! CloudSky Airways offers an instant seat swap feature. Navigate to Manage Booking, enter your PNR, and click "Swap Seats". You can view available cabin seats and instantly switch your seat assignment without cancelling your flight.

How do I request Wheelchair or Special Assistance?

Wheelchair assistance for seniors or passengers with reduced mobility is provided free of charge at all airport hubs. Please request wheelchair assistance at least 24 hours prior to departure by calling our 24/7 toll-free helpline at 1800-209-7755 or emailing concierge@cloudsky.in.

What are the guidelines for infants and expectant mothers?

Expectant mothers with uncomplicated pregnancies may travel up to 36 weeks of pregnancy (or 32 weeks for multiple pregnancies) with a fit-to-fly certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner within 7 days of departure. Infants up to 2 years travel under the lap infant fare with a dedicated 10 kg baggage allowance.

Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

Plans change. We offer hassle-free online cancellations with guaranteed instant refund calculations and immediate seat inventory release.

24-Hour Free Cancellation Guarantee

Cancel within 24 hours of booking for a 100% full refund with zero cancellation fee, provided the flight departure is at least 7 days away from the booking time.

Instant Online Cancellation Workflow

  1. Navigate to the Manage Booking Portal.
  2. Enter your 6-character Reference (PNR) and email.
  3. Review your itinerary and click "Cancel Reservation".
  4. The system automatically computes statutory deductions and your net refund amount in Indian Rupees (₹).
  5. Confirm cancellation: your booked seats are immediately released back to the airline inventory, and a refund transaction is generated.

Refund Settlement Timelines

Refunds are credited back exclusively to the original payment instrument used during booking:

  • UPI / Google Pay / PhonePe: Instant to 24 hours
  • Credit / Debit Cards: 3 to 5 business days
  • NetBanking / IMPS: 2 to 4 business days

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

CloudSky Airways is committed to preserving the privacy of our travelers. All personal information collected during flight search, booking, payment, and web check-in is encrypted via 256-bit SSL transport security.

DGCA Passenger Rights Charter

In accordance with the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) CAR Series M Section 3 guidelines:

  • Flight Delays over 2 Hours: Complimentary refreshments and snacks provided at airport terminals.
  • Flight Cancellation with Short Notice: Choice of alternate flight within 2 hours or 100% full refund without deduction.
  • Denied Boarding (Overbooking): Full refund plus monetary compensation in accordance with statutory guidelines.
  • Baggage Delay or Damage: Swift compensation up to ₹20,000 for checked baggage loss upon verified claim filing.
